> *"True presence needs no announcement. It simply endures."*

A private, bespoke cinematic web monograph dedicated to Merna.

## ✦ Highlights

- **Visual Monograph**: 9 curated high-resolution portraits with editorial chapters and full-screen lightbox exploration.
- **Ambient Stardust Canvas**: Native HTML5 celestial particle system.
- **Atmosphere**: Acoustic instrumental soundtrack (*Golden Brown*).
- **A Sincere Invitation**: Sincere and respectful dialogue options.

---

## 🚀 Live Deployment (GitHub Pages)

To publish live:
1. Go to repository **Settings** > **Pages**
2. Under **Build and deployment**, select:
   - **Source**: `Deploy from a branch`
   - **Branch**: `main`
   - **Folder**: `/ (root)`
3. Click **Save**
4. Your site will be live at: `https://max-fucks.github.io/merna/`
